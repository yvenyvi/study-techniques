BEFORE YOU SETTLE IN

A few good
things to know.

Will this automatically turn a PDF into notes?

You choose the passages that matter and add them to your notes with a page reference. Study Room doesn’t generate AI summaries. Scanned PDFs can be read, but text selection needs a PDF with selectable text; OCR isn’t included.

Where do my notes and files go?

The web app uses this device’s browser storage. The Windows app uses its own local database and PDF folder, independent of browser data. Neither uploads your materials. Export ZIP backups in Settings and keep them somewhere safe.

Can I move between web, Windows, and iPad?

All three apps support the same version-1 ZIP backup format. Transfer a backup manually into an empty workspace before saving preferences or starting a timer. The iPad app is still in development. Automatic sync isn’t included.

Does finishing a timer complete my goal?

You decide when the goal is complete. The timer helps you focus and record progress, but a good study session is about reaching your defined outcome.
